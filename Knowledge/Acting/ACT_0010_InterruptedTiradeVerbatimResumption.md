# ACT_0010: Interrupted Tirade Verbatim Resumption

## 1. Overview & Core Concept
The **Interrupted Tirade Verbatim Resumption** is a comedic dialogue and performance technique illustrating obsessive pettiness. When a character's aggressive verbal assault is interrupted by an unavoidable polite social interaction, the character retains the exact uncompleted insult in short-term working memory, resuming the sentence verbatim the moment the intervening party leaves.

```
[ TIRADE INITIATED ]  --> "దున్నపోతుల్లా తిని..." (Cut off mid-sentence)
        |
[ HIGH-STATUS INTERRUPT ] --> 20 seconds of humble bowing & pleasantries
        |
[ AUTHORITY EXITS ]   --> 180° head snap + instant retrieval:
                          "ఏంటే... ఆ! ఇందాక ఏంటి తిడుతూ ఆపాను? దున్నపోతుల్లా...!"
```

---

## 2. Biomechanical & Behavioral Mechanics

### A. The Kinetic Reversion Snap
1. **Head & Neck Torque:** As the authority figure clears the exit boundary, the actor performs a sudden, rapid cervical rotation (approx 120–180 degrees) toward the original victim.
2. **Mandibular Lock:** The awe-struck slack jaw (`AU26`) from the authority interaction violently snaps shut into clenched mandibular rage (`AU29` + `AU4`).
3. **Weight Transfer:** The actor shifts weight aggressively onto the front foot, re-establishing forward spatial intimidation.

### B. Cognitive Retrieval Hesitation (The Micro-Beat)
To maximize comedic authenticity, the actor inserts a 0.2–0.4 second micro-pause wherein:
- Eyes squint slightly as the character searches for where they left off.
- Vocalizes an exploratory filler (*"ఏంటే... ఆ! ఇందాక ఏంటి తిడుతూ ఆపాను?"*).
- Immediately explodes upon locating the exact interrupted phoneme.

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Directorial Execution

1. **Plant a Distinctive Phonetic Anchor:** The interrupted sentence must cut off on a colourful, memorable word or insult (e.g., *"దున్నపోతుల్లా..."* / *"Water buffaloes..."*).
2. **Sustain the Sincere Interruption:** Ensure the intervening interaction feels complete and genuinely deferential, lasting long enough that the audience forgets the character was scolding someone.
3. **Trigger Immediate Reversion:** Cut to the character standing alone the instant the authority departs, catching the visceral transformation back into an angry tyrant.
4. **Verbatim Phonetic Match:** Ensure the actor matches the pitch, timbre, and acoustic volume of the original pre-interruption sentence.
