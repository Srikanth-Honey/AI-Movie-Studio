# ACT_0011: False Exit Appeasement Baiting

## 1. Overview & Core Concept
**False Exit Appeasement Baiting** is a classic comedic performance trope in domestic and familial scenarios. When faced with an innocuous question or minor slight, the character reacts with hyperbolic offense, physically turning around to leave and threatening extreme hardship (e.g., sleeping at the railway station) to force the other party into desperate appeasement.

```
[ INNOCUOUS QUESTION ]    --> "You said Saturday, why Friday?"
            |
[ 180° FALSE EXIT ]       --> Violent heel pivot + luggage swing
            |
[ MELODRAMATIC RETORT ]   --> "Give it here... I'll go sleep at the station!"
            |
[ DESIRED OUTCOME ]       --> Other party panics, pleads, and offers hospitality
```

---

## 2. Technical Anatomy & Directorial Staging

### A. Biomechanics of the False Exit
1. **The Heel Pivot:** The actor plants the rear heel and torques the torso 180 degrees away from the interlocutor within 0.3 seconds of hearing the trigger line.
2. **Prop Centrifugal Swing:** If carrying luggage or accessories (e.g., travel bag, briefcase), the prop must swing in a wide, exaggerated centrifugal arc across the legs, amplifying the kinetic drama of the departure.
3. **Deceleration & Arrest:** The actor takes only 1 or 2 fast steps before intentionally decelerating, remaining well within earshot so the other party can intervene.

### B. Facial & Vocal Execution
- **Facial FACS:** `AU5` (Upper Eyelid Raiser - wide bulging eyes) + `AU38` (Nostril Flare) expressing theatrical indignation.
- **Vocal Prosody:** High-pitched, aggrieved petulance accelerating through the sentence, ending in an abrupt nasal bark.

---

## 3. Practical Directorial Rules
- **Rule 1 (The Speed of Offense):** The pivot must happen immediately upon hearing the word that triggers the offense; any delay reveals conscious calculation and spoils the instinctive comedy.
- **Rule 2 (The Over-the-Shoulder Delivery):** The retaliatory dialogue should be delivered while facing away or looking sideways over the shoulder.
- **Rule 3 (The Inevitable Yield):** When the other character offers the appeasement beat, the departing character must smoothly abandon the exit with zero friction.
