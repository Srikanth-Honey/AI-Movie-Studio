# HUM_0004: Positional Containment Decree

## 1. Overview & Core Concept
**Positional Containment Decree** is a human behavioral and authority-staging principle observed in high-stress crisis scenarios. 

When facing an imminent threat, untrained individuals naturally exhibit panic-driven kinetic agitation (standing up, pacing, peeking through windows, attempting escape). A dominant authority figure arrests this collective hysteria by issuing an absolute **Positional Containment Decree** — demanding that everyone remain rooted to their exact seats like nailed objects, thereby preserving crossfire corridors and establishing tactical order.

```
[ EXTERNAL THREAT DETECTED ]
              |
[ PANIC / AGITATION ] (Startle rises, pacing, looking out windows)
              |
[ POSITIONAL CONTAINMENT DECREE ] 
("Do not move an inch left or right... sit like you are nailed to the chair!")
              |
[ HYPNOTIC COMPLIANCE ] (Kinetic panic frozen, room becomes a controlled operational grid)
```

---

## 2. Behavioral Dynamics & Vocal Authority

### A. The Verbal Injunction
- **Formula:** Specific spatial restriction + graphic physical metaphor (*"ஒரு இன்ச் கூட லெப்ட் ரைட் நகராமே ஆணி அடிச்ச மாதிரி உட்காந்த இடத்திலயே உட்காந்துருக்கணும்"* / *"Without moving an inch left or right, sit like you are nailed to the spot"*).
- **Vocal Delivery:** Flat, baritone, completely free of vocal tremor or breathlessness.

### B. Suppression of Startle Reflexes
When secondary characters attempt startle rises (e.g., jumping up when car engines rumble outside), the commander immediately issues an explosive, staccato verbal check (*"டேய் எந்திருக்க கூடாதுன்னு சொன்னே உடாரு!"* / *"I told you not to get up, sit down!"*), immediately forcing them back down into submissive compliance.

## 3. Narrative & Practical Staging Utility
- **Clears the Kill Zone:** Ensures innocent family members stay beneath window levels and out of sniper bullet trajectories.
- **Amplifies Contrast:** The rigid immobility of the family heightens the shocking kinetic violence of the arriving assailants when they breach the room.
